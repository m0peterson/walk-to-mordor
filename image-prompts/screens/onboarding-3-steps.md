# Онбординг 3: шаги

- **Где в приложении:** экран, где приложение просит доступ к шагам (Health Connect или датчик активности). Смысл: «каждый твой шаг в реальности это шаг по карте».
- **Формат:** 4:5 вертикальный (Midjourney `--ar 4:5`)
- **Экспорт:** 1440×1800, WebP ~85%
- **Приоритет:** A

## Prompt

```text
A close-up of a pair of large hairy bare hobbit feet walking along a dirt path through fallen autumn leaves, leaving a line of small footprints in the soft earth that stretches back into the distance. Short grass, little mushrooms and wild flowers along the edges of the path. Warm afternoon light, gentle long shadows. Palette: earthy brown, golden and orange leaves, fresh green grass. Playful and friendly: every step counts. Vertical 4:5 composition, the feet in the lower middle, the trail of footprints leading back into the upper part of the frame. Hand-painted storybook illustration, ink and watercolor on warm cream paper, fine sepia pen linework, loose transparent washes, visible paper grain, painterly, not photorealistic, not a 3D render. No text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame or border.
```

## Negative prompt

```text
text, letters, signature, watermark, frame, border, shoes, boots, socks, human feet, photorealistic, photograph, 3d render, CGI, anime, gross, dirty wounds, deformed toes, extra toes, blurry, oversaturated
```

## Заметки

- Генераторы любят рисовать лишние пальцы. Пересчитайте: по пять на каждой ступне.
