# Онбординг 1: начало пути

- **Где в приложении:** первый экран знакомства. Смысл: «дойди от Шира до Мордора пешком».
- **Формат:** 4:5 вертикальный (Midjourney `--ar 4:5`)
- **Экспорт:** 1440×1800, WebP ~85%
- **Приоритет:** A
- **Референс:** [лист персонажей](../characters/character-sheet.md) и эталон стиля

Нижнюю четверть кадра под текст и кнопки приложение может перекрывать, поэтому персонажи стоят чуть выше.

## Prompt

```text
Two small hobbit companions seen from behind, standing on the top of a green hill at sunrise, looking east along a long winding road that crosses green farmland, woods and rivers and disappears toward distant blue mountains. On the far horizon, barely visible, a faint smudge of dark smoke with a tiny red glint. The slender one has dark curly hair, a forest-green hooded cloak and a walking stick; the sturdier one has light sandy curly hair, a grey-brown cloak and a huge backpack with pots and a coil of rope. Both barefoot. The two characters must match the attached reference sheet. Early autumn sunrise, warm golden light, long shadows, dew on the grass. Palette: warm gold, fresh green, soft blue distance. The hopeful beginning of a great journey. Vertical 4:5 composition: the characters a little below the middle, the road leading the eye up to the horizon, calm sky in the upper part, quiet grass in the bottom quarter. Hand-painted storybook illustration, ink and watercolor on warm cream paper, fine sepia pen linework, loose transparent washes, visible paper grain, painterly, not photorealistic, not a 3D render, not a movie still. No text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame or border.
```

## Negative prompt

```text
text, letters, signature, watermark, frame, border, faces, actor likeness, celebrity face, movie still, photorealistic, 3d render, CGI, anime, chibi, shoes, boots, modern objects, blurry, oversaturated, deformed hands
```
