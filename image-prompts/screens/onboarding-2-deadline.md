# Онбординг 2: выбор срока

- **Где в приложении:** экран, где пользователь выбирает срок похода (пресеты 6, 9, 12, 18, 24 месяца, см. [game-rules.md](../../docs/product/game-rules.md#4-старт-и-срок)).
- **Формат:** 4:5 вертикальный (Midjourney `--ar 4:5`)
- **Экспорт:** 1440×1800, WebP ~85%
- **Приоритет:** A

## Prompt

```text
A still life on an old wooden table in a cozy hobbit home with a round window: an unrolled hand-drawn parchment map with mountains, rivers and forests but no readable writing, a brass hourglass with golden sand running through it, a burning candle, a small leather pouch, a walking stick leaning against the table, and a plain gold ring lying on the map. Warm candlelight, soft evening glow through the round window. Palette: warm amber, honey-brown wood, parchment cream, gold. Thoughtful mood: planning the journey and deciding how long it will take. Vertical 4:5 composition, the hourglass in the center, calm darker areas at the top and bottom. Hand-painted storybook illustration, ink and watercolor on warm cream paper, fine sepia pen linework, loose transparent washes, visible paper grain, painterly, not photorealistic, not a 3D render. No text, no letters, no runes, no inscriptions on the ring, no signature, no watermark, no frame or border.
```

## Negative prompt

```text
text, letters, words, runes, inscriptions, glowing writing on the ring, signature, watermark, frame, border, photorealistic, 3d render, CGI, anime, modern objects, clock, smartphone, blurry, oversaturated
```

## Заметки

- Кольцо простое, без надписей и свечения. Огненные буквы на кольце это узнаваемый образ из фильмов, его лучше не трогать.
