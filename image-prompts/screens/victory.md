# Экран победы

- **Где в приложении:** после вехи 23 (Роковая гора), когда маршрут пройден в срок.
- **Формат:** 9:16 вертикальный (Midjourney `--ar 9:16`)
- **Экспорт:** 1440×2560, WebP ~85%
- **Приоритет:** A

Экраны телефонов вытянутее, чем 9:16, поэтому по бокам картинку обрежет примерно на 10% с каждой стороны. Главное держать в центральной части. Верх и низ спокойные: туда ляжет текст поздравления и кнопки.

## Prompt

```text
Far in the distance, a great dark volcano erupting under a sky that is breaking open into a brilliant golden dawn, the black clouds torn apart and blown away by a strong west wind. Far behind it, a huge dark fortress tower crumbling into ruin. In the sky in the middle of the frame, three enormous eagles with broad golden-brown wings fly toward the viewer out of the light; the largest one gently carries two small cloaked figures in its talons. Below, a dark ash land with fading lava. Palette: golden dawn light, warm white, clear sky blue breaking through charcoal clouds, ember red fading away. Triumph, relief, joy. Vertical 9:16 composition, the eagles in the center, calm open sky in the top fifth and calm dark land in the bottom fifth. Hand-painted storybook illustration, ink and watercolor on warm cream paper, fine sepia pen linework, loose transparent washes, painterly and atmospheric, not photorealistic, not a 3D render, not a movie still. No text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame or border.
```

## Negative prompt

```text
text, letters, signature, watermark, frame, border, close-up faces, actor likeness, movie still, film screenshot, photorealistic, 3d render, CGI, anime, dragons, birds of prey attacking, blood, blurry, oversaturated, neon colors
```
