# 16. The Dead Marshes / Мёртвые топи

- **Где в приложении:** карточка вехи 16 (всплывает при достижении точки) и запись в журнале путешествия
- **Формат:** 3:2 (Midjourney `--ar 3:2`)
- **Экспорт:** 1800×1200, WebP ~85%
- **Сезон и время по книге:** начало марта, сумерки
- **Приоритет:** A

Главный объект держать в центральном квадрате: в списке журнала картинка обрезается до 1:1.

## Prompt

```text

Beneath the surface of the nearest pool, faintly visible, the pale still faces of ancient warriors with weed in their hair, eerie but not gory. A narrow path of grassy tussocks winds through the bog. Dusk under a sickly yellow-grey sky; on the eastern horizon, the jagged dark line of distant mountains. Palette: murky olive, sickly yellow-green, grey, pale ghostly white. Haunting and melancholic, not horror. Main subject: the ghostly lights over the nearest pool. Wide 3:2 landscape composition, the main subject inside the central square of the frame, calmer areas toward the edges. Hand-painted storybook illustration, ink and watercolor on warm cream paper, fine sepia pen linework, loose transparent watercolor washes, visible paper grain, soft hand-made edges, classic mid-century fantasy book illustration, painterly and atmospheric, not photorealistic, not a 3D render, not a movie still. No text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame or border.
```
