# 10. Halls of Moria / Залы Мории

- **Где в приложении:** карточка вехи 10 (всплывает при достижении точки) и запись в журнале путешествия
- **Формат:** 3:2 (Midjourney `--ar 3:2`)
- **Экспорт:** 1800×1200, WebP ~85%
- **Сезон и время по книге:** середина января
- **Приоритет:** A

Главный объект держать в центральном квадрате: в списке журнала картинка обрезается до 1:1.

## Prompt

```text
The interior of an immense ancient dwarven hall carved deep inside a mountain: a vast dark space, a floor of polished black stone, rows of colossal pillars carved like the trunks of mighty trees whose stone branches spread across the ceiling in a branching tracery. A single pale shaft of grey daylight falls from a high narrow window far above. In the far distance at the end of the hall, a deep red fiery glow rises from a chasm crossed by a slender stone bridge without any rail. In the foreground, tiny figures with one small lantern, seen from behind. Palette: blue-black and charcoal, cold grey light, deep red and orange glow. Awe and dread, enormous scale. Main subject: the perspective of pillars leading to the red glow. Wide 3:2 landscape composition, the main subject inside the central square of the frame, calmer areas toward the edges. Hand-painted storybook illustration, ink and watercolor on warm cream paper, fine sepia pen linework, loose transparent watercolor washes, visible paper grain, soft hand-made edges, classic mid-century fantasy book illustration, painterly and atmospheric, not photorealistic, not a 3D render, not a movie still. No text, no letters, no signature, no watermark, no frame or border.
```

- Не добавляйте огненного демона: силуэт балрога сразу тянет генератор к кадрам из фильма. Красного зарева над мостом достаточно.
